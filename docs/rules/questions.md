# Questions

PRoof asked one to three short questions about lines you changed. They help maintainers see that you understand the change, which makes review faster.

## How to answer

Reply in each question's thread on the PR. One or two sentences in your own words is enough. Short answers are fine, and your English doesn't matter; what matters is that the answer is about this code.

Unanswered questions don't count against you for the first few days.
